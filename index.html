Our story

A tradition worth bringing into your routine.

MIROSE African Black Soap celebrates the rich heritage of African black soap and the everyday ritual of cleansing and caring for your skin.

Traditional African black soap recipes vary by region and maker. Ingredients may include plantain skins or cocoa pod ash, along with plant-based oils. Always check the ingredient list for the specific soap you choose.

A little about black soap

Skincare, rooted in tradition

A few helpful reminders for choosing and using black soap.

🌿

Ingredients vary

Traditional recipes differ by producer. Read the ingredient list rather than assuming every black soap has the same formula.

🧼

Use as directed

Follow the product label, avoid contact with eyes and test a small area first, especially if your skin is sensitive.

🤎

Care for your skin

Black soap can feel drying or irritating for some people. Stop using it if irritation occurs.
